YouTube Content Automation Prompts

15 prompts for script writing, thumbnail generation, SEO descriptions, and channel growth. Works with Claude, GPT-4, and Gemini.

15 prompts Updated May 2026 YouTube Content Automation
Filter by tool:
You are a YouTube script writer specializing in [NICHE]. Create a 10-minute video script for a video titled "[TITLE]". Structure it with: (1) Hook — first 30 seconds that stops the scroll, (2) Problem — why this matters to the viewer, (3) Solution — step-by-step walkthrough, (4) CTA — what to do next. Use conversational tone, include 2-3 analogies, and add timestamps for each section.
Script Writing Claude GPT-4
Generate 5 YouTube thumbnail concepts for a video about "[TOPIC]". For each concept, describe: (1) Visual composition — what's in the frame, (2) Text overlay — max 4 words, high contrast, (3) Color palette — specific hex codes, (4) Emotional trigger — what feeling should it evoke. Format as a numbered list with detailed descriptions suitable for an image generation AI.
Thumbnail GPT-4 Gemini
Write an optimized YouTube description for a video titled "[TITLE]" about [TOPIC]. Include: (1) First 2 sentences as a compelling summary with primary keyword, (2) 3-5 relevant hashtags, (3) Timestamps for key sections, (4) Links section with 3 related resources, (5) 200-word SEO-optimized body with secondary keywords naturally integrated. Target keyword: [PRIMARY_KEYWORD].
SEO Claude GPT-4 Gemini
Analyze the YouTube channel [CHANNEL_NAME] and create a 30-day content growth plan. Include: (1) Content gap analysis — what topics are underserved in this niche, (2) 12 video ideas with titles optimized for search, (3) Optimal posting schedule based on audience timezone data, (4) Collaboration targets — 5 channels for cross-promotion, (5) Shorts strategy — 3 viral short-form concepts per week.
Channel Growth Claude
Create a YouTube video title optimization report for these 5 draft titles: [TITLE_1] through [TITLE_5]. For each, provide: (1) CTR score prediction (1-10), (2) SEO keyword density analysis, (3) 3 optimized alternatives, (4) Character count and readability score, (5) Recommended title with justification. Use YouTube search autocomplete data patterns.
SEO GPT-4
Write a YouTube community post that drives engagement for a channel about [NICHE]. Include: (1) A question that invites responses, (2) A poll with 4 options, (3) A teaser for the next video, (4) A behind-the-scenes element. Keep it under 300 words. Tone: [CASUAL/PROFESSIONAL]. Include 2 relevant emojis and a clear CTA.
